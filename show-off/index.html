the-eye is an MCP server for macOS. It gives an agent the one thing a screenshot cannot: what the screen showed over time. Every frame, every freeze, the spring behind a motion, and who was late.

This page is doing it to itself. Each tick around the eye is one frame this page drew. Make it miss some.

Is that corner a squircle?

    
A circular arc fits a superellipse with exponent 2. A continuous corner fits well above 2 and reaches further along the sides. Draw one. The server's own readCorner() reads it back off the pixels, the way it reads a window that holds still.

Thirteen tools.

  

    
eye_record
Record a region or an app's window, one row per display refresh.

    
eye_act
Move, click, scroll, pinch, rotate, Force Touch. Records what the app did in answer.

    
eye_drive
A hand that stays on the mouse. One line of words is one unbroken motion, and it presses only what is under it.

    
eye_report
What a viewer would have noticed, worst first, with the cause.

    
eye_probe
Layer geometry and main-thread stalls from inside the app, with stack samples.

    
eye_glance
A look at a window that may be covered. The user is held for about a third of a second.

    
eye_inspect
The accessibility tree of an app, or everything the element under a point exposes.

    
eye_snapshot
One picture of a region, plus what the still frame says about text and corners.

    
eye_peek
The live state of a running recording.

    
eye_lock
Hold the trackpad and keyboard while it drives. Orange on screen means locked.

    
eye_app
List, quit, launch or relaunch the app under test.

    
eye_stop
End a recording and get its report.

    
eye_status
Displays, refresh rates, and what is running.

  






  
Put it under your agent.

  

    claude mcp add the-eye -- node src/index.ts
    
  

  
Needs macOS 14 or later, Node 23.6 or later, and the Xcode command-line tools. The terminal or agent that runs it needs Screen Recording and Accessibility permission. The native helper builds itself on first start.
